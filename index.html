Our story

Started in a home kitchen, still made by hand.

What began as weekend batches for friends turned into a bakery supplying businesses across the country. The recipe hasn't changed, and neither has the way we make it.

01

Proper ingredients

Free-range eggs, real butter browned in-house, Belgian chocolate in chunks, not chips. No palm oil, no shortcuts.

02

Rested 48 hours

Every batch of dough rests for two days. It deepens the toffee flavour and gives that crisp-edge, soft-centre bite.

03

Baked to order

We bake against orders, not forecasts, so nothing sits on a shelf. What arrives with you was dough a day or two ago.
