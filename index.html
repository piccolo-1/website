Our story

Started in Nadia's kitchen, still made by hand.

What began as weekend batches for friends and family grew into a bakery supplying businesses and homes alike. The recipes haven't changed, and neither has the way we make them.

01

Proper ingredients

Real butter, good chocolate in generous chunks, proper pistachio cream. No shortcuts.

02

Rested 48 hours

Every batch of dough rests for two days. It deepens the toffee flavour and gives that crisp-edge, soft-centre bite.

03

Baked to order

We bake against orders, not forecasts, so nothing sits on a shelf. What arrives with you was dough a day or two ago.
